Scanning

    How it works

    Four steps, about half a minute, nothing installed on your machine.

    🔌
    Connect

    Opens a real MCP session over Streamable HTTP, with SSE as a fallback.

    🔍
    Inspect

    Reads every tool, prompt and resource. Flags hidden instructions, bloated descriptions and dangerous capabilities.

    🧪
    Probe safely

    An AI agent tries read-only tools with harmless inputs. A code-level gate blocks anything destructive.

    🏁
    Grade

    Every finding is scored and you get a grade from A to F with the evidence behind it.

    Every grade comes from a real run
